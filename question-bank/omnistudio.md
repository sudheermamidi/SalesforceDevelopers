## Practical Scenario

Build an insurance claim intake OmniScript:

- Step 1: Collect policy and claimant information.
- Step 2: Show policy summary in a FlexCard.
- Step 3: Use DataRaptor to read policy data.
- Step 4: Use Integration Procedure for claim validation and external fraud check.
- Step 5: Create Case and Claim records.
