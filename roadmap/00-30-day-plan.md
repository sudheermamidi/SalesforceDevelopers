# 30-Day Salesforce Developer Interview Plan

## Week 1: Platform, Apex, Data Model

Day 1: Salesforce architecture, metadata, org types, setup navigation, object model.
Day 2: SOQL and SOSL, relationship queries, selective filters, query plan basics.
Day 3: Apex fundamentals, collections, exceptions, DML, transactions.
Day 4: Triggers, trigger frameworks, recursion guards, bulkification.
Day 5: Governor limits and performance debugging.
Day 6: Apex tests, test data factories, mocks, `Test.startTest()` and `Test.stopTest()`.
Day 7: Mini mock interview and hands-on trigger exercise.

## Week 2: LWC, UI, Security

Day 8: LWC lifecycle, decorators, component communication.
Day 9: Lightning Data Service, UI API, wire adapters.
Day 10: Calling Apex from LWC, cacheable methods, imperative calls.
Day 11: Forms, validation, toast messages, navigation.
Day 12: CRUD/FLS, sharing model, secure Apex, security review basics.
Day 13: LWC performance and debugging.
Day 14: Build a real-time case triage console.

## Week 3: Clouds and Industry Skills

Day 15: Sales Cloud process design.
Day 16: Service Cloud case management and Omni-Channel.
Day 17: Marketing Cloud core concepts.
Day 18: Health Cloud data model and care workflows.
Day 19: OmniStudio: FlexCards, OmniScripts, DataRaptors.
Day 20: Integrations: REST, Named Credentials, Platform Events.
Day 21: Scenario interview round.

## Week 4: Agentforce, DevOps, Advanced Scenarios

Day 22: Agentforce concepts: agents, topics, actions, grounding, trust.
Day 23: Agentforce handoff and business process design.
Day 24: Data Cloud basics and AI readiness.
Day 25: CI/CD with SFDX, scratch orgs, unlocked packages.
Day 26: Large data volumes, sharing recalculation, async architecture.
Day 27: Debugging production incidents.
Day 28: System-design mock interview.
Day 29: Behavioral and project storytelling.
Day 30: Full mock interview and revision.
